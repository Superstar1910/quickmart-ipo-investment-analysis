Five questions before you subscribe

① Who receives the money? Existing holders. KES 15bn gross secondary sale; Quickmart receives no new equity.

② Are earnings cash? FY2025 CFO KES 4.83bn; cash capex and lease principal materially reduce shareholder cash flow.

③ Is the dividend sustainable? FY2025 dividends exceeded statutory earnings; growth and distributions compete for cash.

④ Is it cheap versus peers? IPO 14.3x FY2026E reported P/E versus 17.7x international sample median; adjust for risk, scale and leases.

⑤ What does a cash-flow model say? Analyst scenarios imply KES 1.18–9.51; high terminal-value sensitivity, not a firm price target.
