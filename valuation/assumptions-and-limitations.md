The post-IFRS16 FCFF adds back right-of-use depreciation and deducts conventional PPE/intangible capex. Future lease asset additions, lease financing and renewals are not matched by a full roll-forward. Deducting opening lease debt alone does not resolve the economics of future leases. The rental approach applies the same WACC for a diagnostic comparison, although excluding lease debt also calls for review of beta and capital-structure consistency.

The KES 8.65 and KES 6.75 outputs differ by KES 1.90 per share. The latter is not an independently validated lower bound. Arithmetic reconciliation of EV minus net debt does not establish economic validity.

Earlier cash-flow scenarios of KES 1.18 / 5.10 / 9.51 are historical sensitivities and must not be presented as a validated bear/base/bull fair-value range.
