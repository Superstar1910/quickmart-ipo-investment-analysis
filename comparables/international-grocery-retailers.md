# International grocery comparison

The dated international benchmark is 17.7x median FY2026E P/E and 8.9x median EV/EBITDA. Observation date: 21 September 2026. Source: Stanbic Bank Kenya / SBG Securities, Offer Price Valuation Parameters, printed page 3, citing Refinitiv. Consult the [official source link](../sources/official-document-links.md).

The peer universe spans Shoprite, Boxer, SPAR, Label'Vie, Gourmet Egypt, BIM, Lulu, BinDawood, Abdullah Al-Othaim, Spinneys and Al Meera. It includes African, Turkish and Gulf markets; it is not a Kenya-only benchmark. All eleven P/E and EV/EBITDA values in the illustration were checked against the source table during packaging.

Quickmart at KES 7.50 is approximately 14.27x FY2026 management forecast reported earnings. The 12.9x adjusted multiple uses a different earnings basis and should not be substituted without checking peer adjustments.

Applying the peer median directly to KES 0.52575 EPS gives KES 9.305775. Applying a judgmental 20% discount gives KES 7.44462. The illustration's KES 9.29 uses the rounded KES 2.10bn profit forecast. Neither calculation establishes the appropriate multiple for Quickmart.

Differences in country risk, liquidity, store formats, profitability, growth, leverage and lease accounting prevent a simple peer median from becoming an intrinsic value. Regional operating comparisons deserve more attention, but selecting peers solely to obtain a favourable multiple would undermine the analysis.
