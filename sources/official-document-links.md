## Source hierarchy and discrepancy

Treat formal offer documentation as authoritative over promotional summaries. During packaging, the issuer website's generic 'Offer Structure' section referred to a primary component, while its offer introduction and detailed FAQ explicitly stated no new shares and no proceeds to Quickmart. The analysis follows the formal IM and detailed offer terms: a secondary sale of existing shares.
